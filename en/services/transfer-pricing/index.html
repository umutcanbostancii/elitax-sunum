Transfer pricing in Turkey

A transfer pricing review examines dealings between related parties. Start by mapping the entities, transactions and pricing policies relevant to the Turkish company.

At the first meeting

Explain which group companies provide goods, finance or services and how charges are calculated. The local records and the group’s documentation should describe the same transactions.

What to prepare

Prepare intercompany agreements, transaction totals by counterparty, the functional analysis and existing group reports. Confirm the applicable reporting obligations for the period with the adviser.

What the work produces

An intercompany transaction schedule ties the accounting records to the contracts and the pricing analysis. Where an annual report is required, the agreed work should identify the method, documentation and unresolved data requests.
