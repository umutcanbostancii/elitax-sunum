Accounting and financial reporting in Turkey

For a Turkish entity or subsidiary, start with the reporting period, local bookkeeping responsibilities and the information its overseas finance team needs.

At the first meeting

Discuss month-end reconciliations, the group reporting pack, chart-of-accounts mapping and the language of the deliverables. Clarify which party handles each filing or payroll step.

What to prepare

Prepare a sample reporting pack, closing calendar, trial balance and list of existing systems. These help define what can be produced from the books and which adjustments need separate support.

What the work produces

Agree which close schedules, reconciliations and financial reports the finance team needs, their frequency and the person responsible for each handover. Identify payroll or filing work separately in the engagement.
