Full tax certification in Turkey

Full tax certification (tam tasdik) is an engagement with a Turkish sworn-in certified public accountant, known as a YMM. The starting point is the entity, period and returns to be covered.

On this page

At the first meeting

Set out which company the engagement concerns, how its accounts are maintained and who prepares its returns. Discuss the review timetable and how the accountant will communicate missing evidence to the finance team.

What to prepare

Prepare the latest returns, trial balance, corporate records and a list of material transactions. Agree on the scope and document responsibilities before transferring records.

What the work produces

During the year, the YMM reviews the records supporting the corporate tax return and identifies items that need correction or evidence. The agreed year-end deliverable is the certification report for the period covered.
