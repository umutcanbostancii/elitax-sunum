VAT refunds in Turkey

A Turkish VAT refund review starts with the transaction creating the claim. An export of goods and a domestic supply taxed at a reduced rate call for different supporting records.

At the first meeting

Identify the period, supply type and intended refund method. Ask the team to reconcile the figures in the return with the accounting records before assembling the application.

What to prepare

For an export discussion, have invoice and customs references, the VAT return and purchase records available. The applicable evidence depends on the type and date of the transaction.

What the work produces

The working file brings together the return, supporting lists and a record of missing evidence. Where the selected refund route requires YMM certification, the engagement also covers the certification report.
