Statutory audit in Turkey

To discuss a statutory audit, establish the reporting period, legal entity and group structure. The applicable audit framework and eligibility of the appointed auditor need separate checks.

On this page

At the first meeting

Describe ownership, regulated activities, reporting standards and whether the group prepares consolidated accounts. Ask which records and comparative periods the assessment requires.

What to prepare

Bring the latest financial statements and group chart. Check the relevant period’s decision and the proposed auditor’s registration with the Public Oversight Authority before an appointment.

What the work produces

The initial review identifies which entity and reporting period to assess, the records to assemble and the timetable for preparation. Verify the appointed auditor in the KGK register before agreeing a statutory audit.
