Tax advisory in Turkey

Businesses entering Turkey or changing an existing operation can request a review of a specific transaction, filing question or correspondence from the tax authority.

On this page

At the first meeting

Describe the business activity, jurisdictions, contracting parties and timing. If the question concerns a notice, identify the date and issuing authority so the team can assess the next step.

What to prepare

Share a summary of the facts first. Agree on the question, required documents and who will receive the written assessment before sending contracts or taxpayer information.

What the work produces

The engagement sets out the question, relevant facts and records reviewed, together with the written assessment or response agreed with the business.
