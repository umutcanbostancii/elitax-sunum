International tax in Turkey

International tax questions for a Turkish business can involve cross-border payments, group relationships, tax residence or the interaction between Turkish filings and overseas reporting. The relevant facts and tax period should be identified before a conclusion is drawn.

At the first meeting

Set out the countries, entities, payment or transaction type, and the dates involved. Explain whether the question concerns a planned arrangement, an existing filing or a notice received by the Turkish entity.

What to prepare

Prepare the relevant contracts, payment flows, group chart, tax residence information and prior filings. Share only the records needed for the agreed review through a secure channel.

What the work produces

The review sets out the payment or transaction, the jurisdictions involved and the treaty provisions and supporting records to examine before implementing the proposed arrangement.
