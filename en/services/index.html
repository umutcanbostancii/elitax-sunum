Areas of expertise

  • Full tax certification in Turkey

    Full tax certification (tam tasdik) is an engagement with a Turkish sworn-in certified public accountant, known as a YMM. The starting point is the entity, period and returns to be covered.

    ↗

  • VAT refunds in Turkey

    A Turkish VAT refund review starts with the transaction creating the claim. An export of goods and a domestic supply taxed at a reduced rate call for different supporting records.

    ↗

  • Statutory audit in Turkey

    To discuss a statutory audit, establish the reporting period, legal entity and group structure. The applicable audit framework and eligibility of the appointed auditor need separate checks.

    ↗

  • Transfer pricing in Turkey

    A transfer pricing review examines dealings between related parties. Start by mapping the entities, transactions and pricing policies relevant to the Turkish company.

    ↗

  • International tax in Turkey

    International tax questions for a Turkish business can involve cross-border payments, group relationships, tax residence or the interaction between Turkish filings and overseas reporting. The relevant facts and tax period should be identified before a conclusion is drawn.

    ↗

  • Tax advisory in Turkey

    Businesses entering Turkey or changing an existing operation can request a review of a specific transaction, filing question or correspondence from the tax authority.

    ↗

  • Accounting and financial reporting in Turkey

    For a Turkish entity or subsidiary, start with the reporting period, local bookkeeping responsibilities and the information its overseas finance team needs.

    ↗
